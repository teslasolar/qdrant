Scientific Basis

This screening is based on peer-reviewed research by Konomi et al. demonstrating correlation between aluminum fluoride (AlF₃/AlF₄⁻) accumulation in brain tissue and neurodegenerative disorders. The AlF-DETECT system uses dual-energy X-ray subtraction imaging combined with Vision Transformer neural networks and Qdrant vector similarity search to identify characteristic patterns associated with early-stage Alzheimer's disease and autism spectrum disorders.

Reference: Konomi et al., "Aluminum Fluoride Accumulation in Neural Tissue and Association with Neurodevelopmental Disorders" (2023)
